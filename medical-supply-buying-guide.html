A medical supply ordering checklist for clinics in Ghana

26 November 2026

A clear request helps the supplier quote the right item and lets your team compare offers on the same terms. Use this checklist for routine restocking and for equipment requests.

Browse store suppliesSpeak with sales

1. Record the item, unit and quantity

Use the item name on your existing purchasing record. Write the order unit next to the quantity. For example, three boxes of 100 makes it clear that the facility needs 300 units.

2. Include compatibility details

For equipment or accessories, provide the brand, model and any part numbers you have. Separate mandatory specifications from preferences if you can consider an equivalent.

3. Group items by when you need them

Add a needed-by date to each group. Ask which items are ready for pickup or delivery and which have a longer lead time. State whether split deliveries are acceptable.

4. Compare the same scope across quotes

Check whether each quote covers the same pack sizes and quantities. For equipment, confirm whether accessories, delivery, installation, training and warranty are included.

5. Save the supplier confirmation

Keep the agreed specification, order reference, quote and fulfilment details together. This gives a colleague a clear record if they need to follow up.
